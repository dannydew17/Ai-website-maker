Our Story

Rooted in Rehoboth Beach

Coastal Valet was started to give Rehoboth Beach restaurants, hotels, event venues, and residential communities a valet partner that understands the realities of a beach town: tight lots, busy weekends, and guests who expect a smooth arrival every time.
